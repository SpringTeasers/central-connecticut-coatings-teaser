The Shop

A coatings shop in East Hartford

Central Connecticut Coatings, Inc. is a coatings shop at 52 Village Street in East Hartford, Connecticut. We deliver technical solutions, by putting science to work and creating sustainable solutions with a wide range of innovative products and services for markets including residential, industrial and the racing industry.

Our roots are in formulating coatings for heat and wear. President Gene Schaeffer began his career as a chemist developing high temperature coatings, and brings 30 years experience in coating formulation and coating processing to every job that comes through the shop.

Capabilities

What We Coat

Eight coating and surface-preparation services, applied in-house at 52 Village Street.

  • 01

    Aluminum Ceramic Coating

    A superior layer of thermal protection against high temperature corrosion and oxidation, for continuous protection up to 1,375°F (745°C) and intermittent exposure to 1,500°F (815°C). Headers are coated inside and out.

  • 02

    Anti Weld Spatter

    An all purpose guard against welding spatter adhering to metal in unwanted places during the welding process.

  • 03

    Carbon Composite Coating

    Coating for carbon composite components.

  • 04

    Dry Film Lubricants

    Dry film lubrication for parts that need to keep moving.

  • 05

    Powder Coating

    For all types of metal substrates, from steel, aluminum, hot dipped galvanized steel as well as extruded architectural aluminum; resistant to scratches, cracking, peeling, UV rays and corrosion.

  • 06

    Protective Coatings

    Applied to parts and assemblies to isolate and prevent materials from reacting with external gasses, liquids or solid elements.

  • 07

    Sandblasting

    We sandblast all parts prior to coating.

  • 08

    Thermal Barrier Coating

    A coating to insulate the thermal conductivity of the base metal.

  • Parts are sandblasted before coating
  • Headers can be coated inside and out
  • Powder coating capacity is 8ft × 8ft × 20ft
  • Items powder-coated must withstand 450°F
  • Almost any color is available
